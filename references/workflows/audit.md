# Workflow: audit

Audits the single-file cinematic landing page against performance budgets, accessibility rules, and luxury quality bars.

## Steps

1. **Performance Budget Check**:
   - Check total asset payload (<15MB for full sequence, <2MB for initial render).
   - Check image preload mechanism and canvas resize listeners.

2. **Accessibility & Keyboard Navigation**:
   - Verify keyboard focus outlines and `aria-label` attributes on interactive CTA buttons.
